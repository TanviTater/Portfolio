02About

I’m Tanvi Tater, a Computer Science undergraduate at JECRC University, graduating in 2027, with a strong interest in software engineering, problem solving, and building things that actually work.

I’ve solved 900+ Data Structures & Algorithms problems on LeetCode, which has taught me to approach problems with patience, logic, and a habit of asking “okay, but can we make this better?” Beyond competitive programming, I enjoy turning ideas into working products using C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, React, and Streamlit.

I’ve built projects ranging from an interactive DSA visualization platform to data-driven dashboards and Java-based database applications. I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood, debugging the parts that refuse to cooperate, and continuously improving what I build.

Currently, I’m focused on becoming a stronger Software Engineer — sharpening my DSA and computer science fundamentals while exploring web development, systems, and real-world software engineering.

When I’m not coding, you’ll probably find me exploring aviation, working on something new, or wondering why the code that worked five minutes ago suddenly decided to betray me.
